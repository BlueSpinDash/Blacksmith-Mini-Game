How to Play

Strike every square twice. The piece on your current square determines your next move. Extra strikes damage the metal and lower your quality.

1. Tap any square to land the first blow.
2. The chess symbol on the square you are standing on decides where the hammer may go next — the symbol on the destination does not matter.
3. Two strikes forge a square perfectly. A third strike cracks it and costs quality.
4. The board finishes on its own once every square has at least two strikes.

Marks: a brass diamond shows where the hammer stands now; dashed rings show every legal destination. Notches along the bottom of a square count its strikes, and a red number appears once a square is overworked.

Sliding pieces jump over anything in the way — only the destination is struck. There are no captures, no blockers and no timer: this is a route-planning puzzle.

Abandon this piece?

You have already started striking. This will discard your progress.
